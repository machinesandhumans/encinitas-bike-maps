# Cardiff: Coast Hwy 101 cycle track

Chesterfield Dr to the Solana Beach line, about 1.3 miles. Class IV cycle track with wheel stops and flexible posts, installed April 2020 for $954,000 (Coast News, Apr 30, 2025).

Checked = the claim was read in the linked page during this session (Oct 25, 2026). "Summary only" = the page was paywalled or partial, so only its summary was seen.

### March 2024 fatality

Ryan William Currie, 48. Three sources now place him in the **northbound** lane near the **south end**, where the NB track begins:

- the Sheriff's sergeant (above): NB bike lane, closer to the 2800 block, just north of the welcome sign;
- [10News, Mar 19, 2024](https://www.10news.com/news/local-news/north-county-news/advocates-demand-change-along-coast-highway-101-following-cyclist-death): "northbound bike lane ... just south of Cardiff State Beach" (checked);
- [Coast Law Group blog, May 23, 2024](https://www.coastlawgroup.com/blog/2024/05/the-difficult-evolution-of-the-101-bike-lane-continues/): found "near the entrance to the protected bike lane" (checked; a law-firm blog, no source given).

This supports Mark's reading (south NB track entrance) over the state record's "2700 block". It is still not an exact point: none of these names the object he hit. To close it, request the Sheriff's traffic collision report (report number from CCRS 4622805) or the Medical Examiner's investigative report. Small conflict to note: 10News says just after midnight Saturday (Mar 16); the Coast News and CCRS say Mar 17.

## Gaps

1. No bike or pedestrian counts for the stretch, before or after. Crash rates per rider can't be computed.
2. The state database misses most solo crashes; the gap between 11 official and 36 to 42 advocate-counted crashes is mostly this.
3. Advocate lists not obtained, so none of their incidents are matched to records yet.
4. Fatality: exact point and object struck not in any public source found.
5. No city staff report found answering the April 2025 commission data request.
