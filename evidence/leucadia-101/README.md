# Leucadia: Coast Hwy 101 Streetscape

A St to La Costa Ave. Road diet from four lanes to two, roundabouts (Grandview, Jupiter among them), bike lanes, parking changes. Phase 1 (Marcheta to Basil) 2021 to 2022; B-C restripe Jul to Dec 2022; Segment C under construction since Mar 2024.

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| City spokesperson Julie Taber (Coast News, May 2022) | Officials expect the project to reduce crashes for all modes. |
| Fire Capt. Josh Gordon (same) | Trucks getting stuck "virtually doesn't happen"; drivers pull over between cones. |
| Research | Single-lane roundabouts generally cut injury crashes for drivers; for riders the evidence is mixed and depends on whether they share the circle or are routed around it. See [research](../research/README.md). |
| Official CCRS | The yearly crash count during Segment C construction (about 4.5/yr) is close to the pre-project rate (about 4/yr), so far no clear rise. |
