# Santa Fe Dr, west phase

I-5 to Evergreen Dr / Bonita, about two-thirds of a mile past San Dieguito Academy. Raised and planter-protected bike lanes, back-in angled parking, narrowed travel lanes, speed limit cut from 35 to 30 mph. Built Jan 2024 to Apr 2025; cost $4.15M, $3.09M from the state Active Transportation Program (KPBS, Sep 10, 2026).

Plainly: the official record so far does **not** show a rise in bike crashes on the west phase. Only about 13 months of post-completion data exist, 2025 rider details are unpublished, and the two serious injuries cited at council (April and June 2025) were not found in CCRS.

**How to answer:** the strongest case here is about conflict points (driveways and side streets crossing a protected lane), sight lines from back-in parking, and the city's own seven right-hook locations, not about the crash count, which is too short and too thin to show anything yet.
